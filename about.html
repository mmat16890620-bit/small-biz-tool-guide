About Small Biz Tool Guide

Small Biz Tool Guide is a small, independent publication that compares marketing software for small businesses and creators — email marketing platforms, all-in-one marketing suites, and the tools local businesses use to get found and stay in touch with customers.

Our readers are people who run something small and real: a local shop, a salon, a coaching practice, a one-person online business. They don't have an IT department, and they don't have time to read five pricing pages written in SaaS jargon. We read those pages for them, line the facts up side by side, and explain the trade-offs in plain English.

How we research

  • Every price, plan limit, and feature mentioned on this site is taken from the vendor's own official pricing page, help center, or documentation, and each article links to those sources.
  • Articles display the date the facts were last checked. SaaS vendors change pricing frequently, so we revisit articles and correct them when official pages change.
  • We do not publish fabricated hands-on experience. If we have not used a tool in production, the article is framed as a comparison of published specifications — and says so.
  • Vendors cannot pay for placement, ranking, or a favorable verdict on this site.
